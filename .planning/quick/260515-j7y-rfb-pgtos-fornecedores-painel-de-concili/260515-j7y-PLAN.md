<objective>
Implement the RFB "Pgtos Fornecedores" conciliation panel: a new page under the Receita Federal module that cross-references buyer-side supplier payments (pagamentos_fornecedores) with RFB CBS credit status (rfb_creditos). Surfaces the reforma-tributária risk pattern: "supplier was paid but their CBS is not extinto in RFB → buyer may need to recover."

Purpose: Give the fiscal analyst a single screen to identify which paid suppliers have CBS still in `valor_cbs_nao_extinto > 0`, enabling collection actions before claiming credits.

Output: One new backend handler (single GET endpoint with summary + paginated list), one new frontend page mirroring RFBCreditosCBS.tsx patterns, tab unlocked in navigation, route wired in App.tsx.
</objective>
